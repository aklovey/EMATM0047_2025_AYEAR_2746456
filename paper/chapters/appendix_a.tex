\section{Minimal trace and request records}\label{sec:A.1}

The archive retains immutable public-input and parent-trace identifiers, exact segment offsets, answer-exposure decisions, intervention and reconstruction lineage, raw responses, validation decisions, ranking keys and terminal request states. Public prompts and scoring-only material remain separate. Failed and unaccepted items stay in their original denominators; unknown usage is not recorded as zero.

\section{Analysis and evidence records}\label{sec:A.2}

Analysis checks manifest identity and one terminal response per frozen target-condition pair before scoring. It retains failures, joins conditions by target, and reports usage on explicit denominators. The main cohort's numerical-group mapping supports its CPT sensitivity analysis; the fresh cohort remains separate. Parser or demonstration changes require a new version, preserving original outputs.

The electronic supplement is organised into historical experiments, detailed configuration and generation/statistical records, extended audits and cases, and all available construction-cost records. It retains the full Pro-reviewed source manuscript, the numerical comparison figure and editable source files. The original experiment and evidence archives preserve raw responses and records, including details shortened in this dissertation. The source\_manifest.json and electronic evidence ledger map E1--E9 to the underlying sources; publication references are listed in the Bibliography.
