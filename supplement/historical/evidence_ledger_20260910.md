## Historical token amortisation illustration

An illustrative equal-weight token calculation divides 20,610,316 construction tokens by the observed 2,437.33 tokens saved per target, giving 8,456.10 target uses, or at least 8,457 whole uses under those assumptions. This is a resource-count amortisation calculation, not a monetary break-even claim. If completion tokens have weight w relative to prompt tokens, the corresponding threshold is (6,141,543 + w × 14,468,773) / (2,132.61 + w × 304.72). The weights must come from an applicable cost model, and the observed target savings are not guaranteed under a new workload.
