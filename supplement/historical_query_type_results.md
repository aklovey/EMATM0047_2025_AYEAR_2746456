| Query type | FULL_COT | PNS_COT | Strong14 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Average treatment effect | 17 | 15 | 17 |

The per-type totals show a two-question decline for ATE and a two-question increase for NDE in the original PNS condition. Equal totals for another type need not mean identical target outcomes. With only 20 outcome-selected questions per type, these differences are useful for choosing audit cases, but they do not establish that compression systematically helps mediation or harms treatment-effect reasoning.


Source: historical target-level recomputation, evidence E2. These exploratory counts are not a separate confirmatory comparison.
